# lab_FDI_trade
Over the past 10 years, Canada and Mexico have remained the top 2 countries for U.S. exports,
although Mexico has surpassed Canada in this regard. China remained within the top 3, but it
has not significantly grown in U.S. export value as other countries on the list. The UK,
the Netherlands, Germany, and the ROK remain within the top 10. Each has growth in their value
of U.S. exports, but they have also shuffled positions with the UK and the Netherlands rising,
Germany holding position, and Japan and the ROK falling. Hong Kong and Belgium have disappeared
from the list, meanwhile Switzerland and Taiwan have joined the top 10.
